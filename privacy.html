Privacy Policy

At EditSpark, user privacy is one of our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how our platform handles data while you use our image editing tools.

1. Local Processing

EditSpark is designed to process images directly inside your web browser. Most editing operations are performed locally on your device using HTML5 and JavaScript technologies.

Your files are not intentionally uploaded to our servers during normal image processing operations.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect limited technical information such as:

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Anonymous analytics data
  • Website performance information

This information helps us improve our services and user experience.

3. Uploaded Files

Files selected by users are processed locally in the browser whenever possible. We do not intentionally store your photos, signatures, or documents on our servers.

4. Cookies

EditSpark may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality and analytics.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services such as analytics providers, advertising partners, or search tools. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Google Services

If advertisements or analytics services are enabled in the future, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve relevant content and advertisements.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect our website and users. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.

8. Children's Privacy

EditSpark is not intended for children under the age of 13 without parental supervision.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, contact us through our Contact page.
