🔒 Privacy Policy

At EditSparks, accessible from https://editsparks.in, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by EditSparks and how we use it.

1. Client-Side Processing (Zero Data Upload)

Unlike traditional online photo editors that transfer your sensitive images, signatures, and documents to remote cloud servers, EditSparks operates 100% locally inside your web browser. When you upload a photo to compress or resize, your device processes the file directly using browser JavaScript memory.

Your photos never leave your mobile or desktop computer. We do not store, copy, view, or transmit any of your images to external databases.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Since EditSparks requires no user registration, sign-up, or profile setup, we do not collect personal identification details such as your full name, phone number, address, or financial credentials.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

EditSparks uses standard browser storage mechanisms solely to optimize local workspace performance. We do not use persistent tracking cookies to profile individual user behaviors across third-party networks.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

We may utilize standard web analytics services (such as Google Search Console or basic traffic metrics) to monitor site health, technical crashes, and page responsiveness. These tools collect anonymized log details such as browser types, referring pages, and access timestamps without linking them to identifiable personal information.

5. External Website Links

Our website may contain links to official examination portals, government job websites, or utility references. Please note that EditSparks has no control over the content, privacy practices, or policies of external third-party sites.

6. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. EditSparks is a general utility site for educational, exam registration, and document editing purposes, and does not knowingly harvest any personal data from minors.

7. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
