📜 Privacy Policy

At EditSpark, accessible from our main dashboard, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by EditSpark and how we use it.

🛡️ Zero Server Upload Policy (100% Local)

Your digital privacy is our core mission. EditSpark operates as a pure client-side application. This means when you upload an image to compress, resize, or crop:

  • The image is loaded directly into your browser's local HTML5 Canvas memory.
  • No files or data are ever transmitted, sent, or saved onto external servers or databases.
  • All image processing calculations take place strictly on your local computer or smartphone.

📊 Log Files & Analytics

EditSpark follows a standard procedure of using static hosting platform metrics. These logs do not contain any personally identifiable information, your images, or document data. They only track general parameters like browser type, internet service provider (ISP), and date/time stamps to help optimize website loading speeds.

🍪 Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, EditSpark may use standard cookies to store basic user interface preferences (such as dark mode preferences or tool layout configurations) to enhance the user experience on subsequent visits.
