What is Image Compression?

Image compression is the process of reducing the file size of a graphics file in bytes without degrading the visual quality of the image to an unacceptable level. This is accomplished by mathematically encoding the data more efficiently, or by selectively discarding unnecessary details that the human eye cannot easily perceive. On EditSpark, we utilize advanced client-side processing algorithms to scan through your images and adaptively scale down high-frequency pixel regions, enabling quick size transformations to precise specifications like 20KB or 50KB seamlessly.

Why Should You Compress Images?

Large uncompressed images pose substantial challenges across the digital landscape. Firstly, massive file sizes slow down web page loading times, severely hurting user experience and negatively impacting search engine optimization (SEO) performance. Secondly, a majority of central government portals, educational councils, and banking recruitment agencies enforce strict file size thresholds for document uploads. Compressing images helps ensure that your identity proofs, educational marksheet scans, and signatures meet compliance criteria without sacrificing legibility.
