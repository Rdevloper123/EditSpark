What is Passport Size Photo?

A passport size photo is a specialized image format used globally for official identification cards, travel visas, and government forms. Unlike random portrait snapshots, these photos have to follow strict measurement guidelines and specific layout boundaries. Getting these proportions right ensures that your identity applications pass automated biometric checks smoothly without facing unexpected rejections.

Indian Passport Photo Size Requirements

For official document submissions in India, the most common layout choice is the standard 35x45 passport photo layout, which measures exactly 3.5 cm in width and 4.5 cm in height. Your face should cover around 70% to 80% of the picture frame area, with your eyes looking directly into the camera. The background should be kept plain white or very light off-white without any visible shadows or distracting objects behind you.
