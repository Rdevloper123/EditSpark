Common Image Cropping Mistakes to Avoid

To avoid portal rejections, keep these common cropping mistakes in mind:

  • Over-Cropping Signature Lines: Trimming too close can cut off the loops of letters like 'g', 'j', or 'y'. Always leave a small margin around your signature.
  • Uneven Shoulder Alignment: For passport photos, ensure your shoulders are level and centered within the crop frame to keep the portrait balanced.
  • Improper Source Aspect Ratios: Manually squeezing a photo into specific dimensions can stretch or distort your face. Always use an aspect-locked tool.
  • Low-Light Source Images: Cropping a dark photo reduces clarity and can make signatures unreadable. Use well-lit images on clean white backgrounds.
