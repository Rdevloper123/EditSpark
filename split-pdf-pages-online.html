Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is it safe to split PDF files on EditSparks?

Your PDF is processed in your browser using client-side code. The file is not intentionally uploaded to an EditSparks server for splitting. Keep a backup of important documents.

How can I extract specific pages from a PDF?

Select your PDF, choose Extract Specific Pages, and enter page numbers or ranges such as 1, 3, 5-7. Click Split & Download PDF to save the selected pages in one PDF file.

Can I save every PDF page as a separate file?

Yes. Choose Extract All Pages to create an individual PDF for each page. The separate PDF files are packaged into a ZIP archive for download.
