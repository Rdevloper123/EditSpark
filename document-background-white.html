How to Make Document Background Pure White Online?

Have you taken a photo of a document, assignment, or signature from your mobile phone, only to find it looks dark, grey, or full of shadows? Our free online Document Cleaner tool is designed to fix exactly that problem in seconds.

Key Features:

  • Remove Shadows Safely: Intelligently filters out uneven lighting and mobile camera shadows without erasing your original handwriting or printed text.
  • Pure White Background: Boosts the background colors to clean 100% white (#FFFFFF), making it ready for official submissions, prints, or digital scanning records.
  • Darken Signatures & Text: Increases the contrast of ink, stamps, and text marks so that signature transparency or black-and-white documents look sharp and readable.
  • 100% Privacy Guaranteed: All document processing happens directly within your web browser. Your sensitive receipts, forms, or IDs are never uploaded to any cloud server.

Steps to Clean Your Scanned Files:

1. Upload your photo or scanned document using the upload box above.
2. Adjust the Background Whiteness slider to clear away grey shades and dark shadows.
3. Adjust the Text / Signature Darkness slider to bring back deep black contrast to your text lines.
4. Click on 'Download' to instantly save your clean image.
