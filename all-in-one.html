How to Resize and Compress Images for Govt Forms?

  1. Upload Photo: Click the dropzone to select your passport photo or signature image.
  2. Manual Crop: Click 'Crop Karen' to perfectly frame your face or signature block.
  3. Set Dimensions: Enter custom Width and Height in pixels (e.g., 350x450 PX for SSC).
  4. Target KB Size: Type in your maximum file size limit (e.g., 20 KB or 50 KB).
  5. Process & Download: Hit apply to safely convert and save your completely optimized image.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How can I compress an image below 50KB or 20KB?

Simply upload your photo, enter '50' or '20' in the Target Max File Size block, and click process. Our algorithm loops compression parameters to hit your metric perfectly.

Is it safe to upload my passport photo and signatures to EditSpark?

Yes, it is entirely safe. EditSpark is a client-side utility tool. The image processing occurs in your browser engine via HTML5 Canvas; your data never transfers across the internet.
